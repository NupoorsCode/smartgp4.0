Your journey

Your weight loss journey is deeply personal

There is no single reason people want to lose weight. Perhaps you want to feel more comfortable in yourself. Perhaps you are ready to make a change after years of trying different approaches. Perhaps your weight has started to affect your health and your everyday life.

Whatever brings you here, care should begin with understanding you — not simply your weight. SmartGP takes a clinical, considered approach to weight management, combining medical expertise with care that fits around your life.

Care that listens first We assess your full health history, your metabolic profile and your routine before anything is prescribed.

A simple journey

Weight loss care, without the guesswork

A clear, regulated five-step pathway from your first question to sustained, clinician-supported progress.

  1. 01

    Tell us about yourself

    A short assessment covering your health, your routine and what you want treatment to do. About eight minutes.

  2. 02

    Meet your clinician

    A private video consultation with a UK-registered prescriber. Unhurried, and yours.

  3. 03

    Talk through the options

    They assess your circumstances and discuss whether medical treatment is appropriate at all.

  4. 04

    Receive your treatment

    If it is clinically appropriate, your prescription is issued and dispensed by SmartRx with discreet delivery.

  5. 05

    Continue with support

    Ongoing clinical review, dose guidance and someone to ask when something changes.

Nothing is prescribed without a thorough UK clinical assessment.

Clinical care

Your health comes first. Always

Weight management is about a great deal more than choosing a medicine. Our clinicians look at the whole context of your life — your medical history, what you already take, and how your days actually run — before discussing any treatment plan with you.

  • Registered UK clinicians Care delivered by prescribers registered with the GPhC, GMC or NMC, and checkable on the public register before you book.
  • Evidence-informed decisions Prescribing follows national guidance, safety screening and documented contraindications.
  • Private video consultations Speak openly with your clinician from your own home, at a time you chose.
